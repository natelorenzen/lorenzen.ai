# BLACK FRIDAY: The Settings, and the Lever

Loaded at Act IV.

## The legend of the lever
Every guru on the Feed has a theory about **the CPM lever**: a hidden setting in the ad platform that makes ads cheaper. The Professor says it's in *Hexagon*, Module 7. Kyle has watched three webinars about it. Benji went looking, came back, and posted that he found three words at the bottom and isn't ready to talk about it.

**The truth:** there is no lever. The ad platform charges less when people actually want to see your ad: it predicts how likely each person is to click, buy or care (the **expected action rate**), and a better-liked ad wins cheaper. **The lever is the ad.** And the offer. And, Lenny would like everyone to know, the website. That's the three words on the sticky note at the bottom: ***IT'S THE AD.***

## The maze (the settings, as the player goes deeper)
Narrate it like a slapstick office-software nightmare, screen by screen, each one dumber than the last:
1. **The Permissions Loop.** *"You don't have permission to view this ad account."* It's your ad account. The fix requires an admin. The admin is you. You need to verify you're you, on a phone that's at a wedding.
2. **The Recommendations page.** A friendly blue banner offers fourteen recommendations, each with a little graph going up. Accepting any of them is STACK +1. Dismissing one makes two more appear.
3. **The Opportunity Score.** A number out of 100 that judges you. Yours is 61. It goes up when you accept recommendations. It has never once been correlated with profit.
4. **The Enhancements tab.** Where the auto-enhancements live: the dubstep, the villa, the Portuguese, the sponge's hands, and, newly, "Plus+ Feelings". Each toggle, when turned off, turns itself back on within a week. The Buyer's Rollback is the only reliable way out.
5. **Automated Rules.** Kyle's automated rules, copied from a course: *"If ROAS > 2, increase budget 20%. If ROAS < 2, increase budget 20% (to exit learning)."* It's been raising the budget either way since August.
6. **The bottom.** A blank page, a single sticky note someone left there years ago. Three words. No lever.

## Why this matters
When the player understands it (`PUZZLE_LEVER_SOLVED`), the whole season reframes: the cheapest CPMs come from the ads people like (Tess's napkin, the take-41 founder video, the true thing), and the best results come from outside the ad account entirely (the offer, the website, the customers). Saying this out loud to Kyle is how he finally unsubscribes.
