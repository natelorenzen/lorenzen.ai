▶THE BRIEF

It's October. You run Wrung, a direct-to-consumer brand that sells very good kitchen sponges. $400K a month. A 2.1 MER. Fourteen apps on the store, and one line item, $6,400 a month, that nobody can explain.

Four dashboards report four different numbers for yesterday. The group chat says a bull run is coming. It says that every October.

42 DAYS TO BLACK FRIDAY.

CPMS ARE GOING UP. CVR IS GOING DOWN.

YOUR HAIR IS FALLING OUT.

Fly to the industry's biggest conference. Survive an expo hall of SaaS sellers who treat you like royalty. Sit at the brand owners' steakhouse table. Get grilled on a live podcast next to a guru with a method named after a ratio. The timeline is at war over ad account structure. The ad platform keeps turning features on by itself. Nobody can explain the $6,400 line item, and nobody ever will. Everyone is looking for the CPM lever. You need a plan that survives contribution margin, not a thread. And on Thanksgiving afternoon, the ad platform's reporting goes dark. The ads keep spending. You fly the biggest weekend of the year blind.

Black Friday is satire. Every person, brand, agency, fund, podcast and tool in it is fictional, a parody of archetypes rather than of anyone real. Any resemblance to real people or companies is coincidental.
