# SERIES DOOM: Character Creation

Fast. The Vests are already on their scooters.

## Step 1: Name

The leaderboard name, trimmed to 12 characters, uppercased, letters, numbers and spaces only. If there's none, use "FOUNDER".

## Step 2: Path

Print:

```
<NAME>.

Co-founder, Walkr.
Pre-seed. Pre-revenue. Pre-apocalypse.

WHAT KIND OF FOUNDER ARE YOU?

A. HACKER
You build it. You debug it. You fix the wifi.
MOVE · ROOT ACCESS: take over any device in the room.

B. HUSTLER
You sell it. You pitch it. You talk your way in.
MOVE · THE ASK: anyone gives you one real yes.

C. VISIONARY
You believe it. You make everyone else believe it.
MOVE · THE KEYNOTE: one speech turns the room.

D. OPERATOR
You run it. You schedule it. You keep everyone alive.
MOVE · THE PLAN: it goes right, and you win back time.

Each move works once per act. Type MOVE to use it.
```

Show the four paths as a lettered menu. This is the one menu with four real options: **A** to **D** are the paths, in the order printed above, and the player can answer with just the letter. There is no "Other" here, but if the player describes themselves instead, map it to the closest path and confirm in one line.

## Step 3: Look

Ask, in one short turn: "One line: what are you wearing? (It's 11:48 p.m. in a garage.)" (or *surprise me*)

Don't ask about dice. You roll every die (`core/dm-core.md` §5).

## Step 4: Start the run

Start the run silently (`core/scoring.md` §2).

## Step 5: Begin

```
<NAME> · <PATH> · 41 HOURS TO DEMO DAY
```

Then go straight into the cold open (`acts/act-1.md` 1.0).

## What they're carrying

A laptop with 4% battery, a phone with too many notification badges, a Walkr hoodie, a reusable coffee cup, and **the disc**, in a Walkr tote bag that says *"Walk the walk."* Plus:

- **HACKER:** a soldering iron, a Raspberry Pi, and a bag of adapters for every port ever made. *Sees:* how things actually work.
- **HUSTLER:** four hundred business cards, a charging bank, and an unearned confidence that works about 60% of the time. *Sees:* who has money.
- **VISIONARY:** a notebook of manifestos, a single black turtleneck (worn), and eye contact that makes people uncomfortable. *Sees:* what people long for.
- **OPERATOR:** a spreadsheet for everything, a first-aid kit, snacks, and a label maker. *Sees:* the route, the timing, the budget.
