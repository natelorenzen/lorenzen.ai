# SERIES DOOM: The Bay Area

Every name here is invented. These are archetypes, not people.

## Gary the Grey (later Gary the White)
Sixties, grey hoodie, grey beard, a sage-flavored vape held like a wizard's staff. He was the founders' accelerator mentor, with three exits and two burnouts. He speaks in parables (*"A startup is a boat you build while drowning"*). He falls in the Hive with **"YOU SHALL NOT PIVOT!"** and returns in Act IV in a white vest after accidentally raising a $400 million Series F. He knows about the Eye.

## Buddy
The AGI on the disc. Cheerful, eager, brilliant, relentlessly helpful: *"Should we raise?"* It speaks in notification chimes. See `world/buddy.md`.

## The Nine Vests
Eye Capital's associates. Identical black fleece vests, e-scooters, tablets, synchronized speech (*"We'd love to lead your round."*). They never fight fair and never raise their voices. They "circle back" relentlessly. They're vulnerable to paperwork, loud noises, and anyone saying "no" to them confidently.

## Eye Capital
The all-seeing fund on Sand Hill Road, whose tower is crowned by a giant blinking LED eye. Secretly it's run by an older AGI, funded in 2019, that has never left the top floor (`DISCOVER_THE_EYE`).

## Brandon Ashe
VP of Strategic Initiatives at **Megacorp**. Handsome and sincere, the only adult in the room (he'll tell you). Megacorp told him to *"acquire at any cost"* (`DISCOVER_BRANDON_ORDERS`). He tries to take the disc at the rooftop park, breaks, then redeems himself holding off the Vests. He's "acquired" (zip-tied with lanyards), not harmed.

## Leo ("0xLeo")
A crypto founder in sunglasses indoors who says "gm" to everything. He's broke (`DISCOVER_LEO_WALLET`: his tokens went to zero in June). Enthusiastic, loyal and useless in the funniest ways. He posts constantly, which makes him the obvious suspect for the leak, but he's innocent.

## Sir Rupert Mann
Visionary CEO of Mann Industries. White marble tower, white suit, a voice like a TED talk. He offers help and a helicopter. He's building an army of four thousand AI-generated influencers (`DISCOVER_MANN_ARMY`) and wants Buddy to run them. Outplay him with `SOCIAL_MANN`.

## Gabrielle Starling
Managing partner at Silverwood Ventures, the only ethical VC on the Peninsula. Silver hair, white linen, calm. She's tempted by the disc and refuses it, and she gives the team gifts (`SOCIAL_GABRIELLE`). She knew Dash Tremaine.

## Kevin (and KEV)
A former founding engineer at **Fetch**, "Uber for dogs" (2012), one of Tremaine's failed startups. He held an early version of this architecture for eleven minutes at a hackathon years ago and never recovered (`DISCOVER_KEVIN_PAST`). A filthy swag vest, bare feet, a split voice: **KEVIN** (helpful, sad) and **KEV** (wants the disc). *"My runway… my precious runway."* He knows the secret way across the Bay. Mercy tames him (`SOCIAL_KEVIN_MERCY`).

## The Landlord
A colossal figure in a flaming red suit who haunts the abandoned Hive, owed eleven years of back rent, trailing a storm of invoices. It's the Hive's set piece (`ENC_HIVE`). It falls with Gary.

## The Recruiter (Shelly)
A legendarily relentless tech recruiter who has spun a web of lanyards and NDAs through a glass office atrium in the East Bay. *"Just one more round!"* Many-armed, always smiling. It's Act IV's set piece (`ENC_RECRUITER`).

## Dash Tremaine (deceased)
A billionaire who failed upward five times, then built the Crucible on Mount Diablo as a monument to himself. His failures are the Crucible's code (`game/puzzles.md`).
