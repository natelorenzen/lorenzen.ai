# THE GLASS CITY: Confrontations

Spy violence is fast, ugly and rare, and every fight raises Heat. Most confrontations are won by leaving: a vanished trail, a locked door, a lie that holds. When fists or guns come out, it should feel like something went wrong.

## Rules

1. **Open with the situation:** who, where, what they want, and one usable detail (a glass roof, a fire alarm, a tram bell, a boiler valve).
2. **Three approaches, as a lettered menu** (A, B, C, plus D. Other) at the turning point (`core/dm-core.md` §3): **stand** (fight, hold, protect), **evade** (run, hide, lose them, a disguise), and **turn the ground** (an alarm, a blackout, shattered glass, a crowd, a steam valve). Each has a different price: stand costs wounds and heat, evade costs time, gear or separation, and turn the ground costs collateral and noise.
3. **Roll the d20 at the turning point**, usually once or twice per confrontation (`core/dm-core.md` §5). A miss by 1 to 4 costs a wound or +1 heat (`rules.md` §3).
4. **Enemies value survival.** Snatch teams want the player alive. Office security wants an arrest, not a body. Kell's men are the only ones who shoot to kill.
5. **Every confrontation must cost or reveal:** a wound, heat, lost gear, a companion's trust, or a secret.
6. **Paths pay off.** The **Operative** reads the fight before it starts and ends it fast. The **Ghost** always sees one more exit. The **Diplomat** can stall or split the enemy with words. The **Analyst** spots the pattern (who's in charge, what they're protecting) and can turn it.
7. **Escalation:** a failed sneak, a blown cover, or heat reaching 5 triggers a short 2-to-4-decision fight or chase with the same menu. It's unscored.
8. `_SURVIVED` means the player lived through it; `_CLEVER` means an ingenious resolution, and earns both.

## ENC_ARCADE: The Glass Galleries · SET PIECE (Act I)
- **Enemies:** a three-man Directorate snatch team. They want the player alive, and they want to know who the player is meeting.
- **Terrain:** a long glass-roofed arcade at night, with shuttered shops, cast-iron columns, a flower stall, the café's back door, a tram stop at the far end, and puddles reflecting everything.
- **Menu example:** "Take the leader down hard before the others close." / "Duck through Café Oriel's kitchen and out the back." / "Kick over the flower stall's gas lamp and vanish into the smoke and shouting."
- **Reveals:** caught men carry orders that show someone knew the player's face in advance. Afterward, heat is at least 1.

## ENC_RAID: The Raid · SET PIECE (Act II)
- **Enemies:** four Office security men with an arrest warrant (they won't shoot first), and four Directorate men up the service stair (they will, if cornered). Neither team knows about the other.
- **Terrain:** the Meridian's fourth floor (or wherever the player sleeps), with a main stair, a service stair, a lift, a laundry chute, the fire alarm, and the **lobby's glass roof** outside the window.
- **Menu example:** "Hold the corridor corner and make them come to you one at a time." / "Out the window and across the glass roof in the rain." / "Pull the fire alarm and let both teams run into each other in the smoke."
- **Reveals:** the warrant names the player as CARDINAL (`DISCOVER_THE_FRAME`). Afterward, heat is at least 3.

## ENC_GLASSHOUSE: The Glasshouse · SET PIECE (Act IV)
- **Enemies:** Major Kell and four men. They want the courier alive and Colonel Voss quietly dead.
- **Terrain:** the Victorian hothouse at midnight: palms, giant ferns, a fern pool, catwalks, thousands of panes, steam boilers underneath, sprinkler valves, and rain on the glass.
- **Menu example:** "Cover Voss and hold the fern pool while they come through the palms." / "Drop into the boiler tunnels and come out on the hillside." / "Open every steam valve and fill the glasshouse with white."
- **Reveals:** Kell's orders came through Ashby. Whether Voss survives decides who holds the city end of the bridge.

## ENC_BRIDGE: The Glass Bridge (Act V)
See `acts/act-5.md` 5.2. The confrontation depends on who is there: Ashby at the Concord gate, Kell at the city end, Voss mid-span, a betrayal coming due, or Katya missing. Use the three-approach lettered menu at the turning point, but **never at the final choice**.
