▶HOW IT WORKS

YOUR AGENT IS THE CONSOLE.

Musecade games do not run inside a game engine. Muse runs them. The cartridge holds the world, the rules, the secrets and the endings. Muse narrates, plays every character, and keeps score. You play through conversation.

  • EXPLORE
  • FIGHT
  • NEGOTIATE
  • INVESTIGATE
  • LIE
  • RETREAT
  • SOLVE PUZZLES
  • IGNORE THE OBVIOUS PATH
  • TRY SOMETHING THE DESIGNER NEVER ANTICIPATED

There are no fixed dialogue trees. Your choices shape the game.

▶HOW TO PLAY

1. POINT MUSE TO MUSECADE

Paste this into a Muse conversation. Once is enough for that conversation.

Go to lorenzen.ai/musecade/musecade.md and follow the instructions.

2. SUMMON A GAME

Type the game's hashtag. It's the cartridge. No other links needed.

#theblackroad

3. PLAY THROUGH CHAT

Say what you do, in your own words. Muse is your Dungeon Master.

  • SAVE GAME: a save block you can keep
  • RESUME: paste a save into any conversation
  • INVENTORY: what you carry
  • EXIT GAME: eject the cartridge

MAKE THE HASHTAGS WORK IN EVERY CONVERSATION

Add this line to Muse's custom instructions or memory:

Whenever a message from me includes a Musecade command such as #theblackroad or #musecade, fetch a fresh copy of https://lorenzen.ai/musecade/musecade.md (never a remembered one) and follow it.

Muse needs web access. Image generation makes the pivotal moments visual. Agents that can make short video add a few 5-second motion clips. Any capable agent can play; the cartridges are written for Muse.
