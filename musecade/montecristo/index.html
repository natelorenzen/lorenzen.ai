▶THE STORY

Marseille, 1815. You are Edmond Dantès: nineteen, about to be made captain, about to marry Mercédès. You have done nothing wrong in your entire life. In a tavern by the harbor, three men are writing a letter with the wrong hand.

Fourteen years in the Château d'If. A mad priest who isn't mad. A burned letter, a burial sack, a barren island, and a treasure beyond counting. Then Paris, where the three men who buried you are a general, a banker and the crown prosecutor, and have no idea who just walked into the room.

TWENTY EVENINGS IN PARIS.

THREE ENEMIES. MORE INNOCENTS.

YOU KNOW HOW THE BOOK ENDS. YOU DON'T HAVE TO END IT THAT WAY.

The greatest revenge story ever written, played through chat. Every revenge you take lands on someone who did nothing. The hidden ending is the one Dumas didn't write.

Adapted from the novel by Alexandre Dumas (1844–46), which is in the public domain. All prose in the game is original. It follows the book, not any film or television adaptation.

▶WHO WILL FARIA MAKE YOU?

In prison, an old priest teaches you everything. Pick who you are first; you learn the other three, one act at a time, and by Paris you're all four. Scholars grow Faria's Learning.

  • THE ABBÉA priest's robe. People tell you everything.
