# DON'T SPLIT UP: The People of Hollow Pines

Every name here is invented.

## Jack Hollow
Seven feet of scarecrow in a rotted brown farm coat and gloves stuffed with straw. His head is a huge carved jack-o'-lantern, and the candle inside it is the only part of him that's warm. One hand holds an old railroad lantern; the other holds a harvest sickle he never actually uses on anyone. **He never runs.** He walks, slowly, and is always somehow ahead of you. He doesn't speak until Act IV, and when he does it's in a dry, rustling voice like leaves in a gutter. He **takes** people who are alone and playing their part (`rules.md` §2 and §3). See `world/jack-hollow.md`, loaded in Act IV.

## Earl Grubbs
Seventies, overalls, a feed cap, a toothpick. He runs the **Last Chance Gas & Bait** on County Road 9. Every Halloween he delivers The Warning (*"You kids ain't goin' up to Crescent Lake, are ya?"*) and he reads it **off index cards** taped under the counter (`DISCOVER_EARL_CARDS`). The Committee pays him forty dollars and a ham. He has done it for ten years, and he hates it. Talked to like a person, he goes off script and tells one true thing (`SOCIAL_EARL`).

## Darlene Mutch
Twenty-seven. In 1979 she was a seventeen-year-old counselor at Camp Crescent, and the only one who walked out of the woods at sunrise. Nobody believed her. She's lived alone in the camp's old mess hall for ten years with flare guns, tripwires, a ham radio, a cork board of string and clippings, and a lot of opinions. Grim, furious, very funny, and waiting a decade to say "I told you so". She knew Hank Pruitt, Dale's grandfather. Earning her trust is `SOCIAL_DARLENE`; her story is `DISCOVER_DARLENE_STORY`.

## Sheriff Buck Dunlap
Fifties, mustache, aviator sunglasses at night, a cruiser full of traffic cones. He has never once believed a teenager, and he's proud of it. He is **not** on the Committee: they keep him busy directing festival parking every Halloween, and he's never noticed the pattern. Making him believe is `SOCIAL_SHERIFF`, and it gets the town's fire bell, a radio, and a squad car.

## Mayor Peggy Pumphrey
Sixties, a cardigan covered in embroidered pumpkins, a whistle, a clipboard, and terrifying good cheer. She chairs the **Halloween Committee** (`DISCOVER_THE_COMMITTEE`). She has never personally hurt anyone, and she will tell you so. *"Do you know what Hollow Pines was before Jack? A bypass."* Outplaying her is `SOCIAL_MAYOR`. She offers the player a deal (`SEASON PASS`).

## Lyle Pettibone
Forties, nervous, a utility vest full of gaffer tape. The Committee's effects man. He once worked on *Hollow Night Part II* (craft services, then fog). He dresses Cabin 13 every year: the fog machines, the speaker in the chimney, the corn-syrup blood, the book. He hides in the cabin's crawlspace on Halloween night to "run the show", and he is very, very sorry.

## The rest of the Halloween Committee
**Agnes Croft**, the town librarian, who keeps the archive and the minutes in a pumpkin-shaped binder. **Harlan Beck**, who owns the hayride and the corn maze. Both are polite, cheerful, and complicit.

## Hank Pruitt (deceased)
Dale's grandfather. Director of Camp Crescent in 1979. He found out about the Committee that night, and they bought his silence with a threat against his family. He kept every flyer, every year, in a shoebox, and never had the courage to stop it.

## The 1979 counselors
Six camp counselors went into the woods on Halloween 1979. Darlene came out. The other five were found the next Halloween, asleep in the Pumpkin Patch, one year older and with no memory of the year. The town said they'd run away and come back. They all moved very far away.
