# ACT I: THE ROAD IN

*A thump under the van, the last gas station, a warning read off a card, and a stranger with a camcorder.* Target: 10 to 14 minutes, 7 to 10 decisions. Halloween, 7:40 p.m. to about 9:30 p.m.

**Route** (each scene's goal is the status line's `NEXT`): find out what you hit → get gas at the Last Chance → *decide about Courtney* → drive the lake road → **walk into Cabin 13**.

---

## 1.0 COLD OPEN: THE THING IN THE ROAD (the tutorial)

**Open with action, straight after the path tag.** It's easy, nobody gets hurt, and it teaches the game in 4 or 5 decisions.

**The scene:** County Road 9, a two-lane blacktop through pine woods, fog lying in the low spots. Inside **the Mothership** (Dale's van: shag carpet, a wizard airbrushed on the side, a cassette of party music that keeps slowing down by itself). **Dale** is driving with his werewolf mask on his forehead. **Wendell** is in the back with his notebook, explaining that the radio static "is a classic omen, statistically".

**THUMP.** Something goes under the front wheels. Dale stomps the brake. The van stalls. The engine ticks. In the red glow of the taillights, thirty feet back, **something is lying in the road**. It's shaped like a person, sort of. It isn't moving.

- **Dale**, already unbuckling: *"Okay. Okay. I'll be right back."*
- **Wendell**, very fast: *"Nobody says that. Nobody ever says that. Dale. DALE."*
- **Path spotlight**, one line for this path only:
  - JOCK: *the van's sliding door sticks, but one good shove and it's open, and you could carry anything back to the van at a run.*
  - NERD: *a stall in the woods, a body in the road, a friend who wants to check alone. You've seen this one. It ends with the body being gone.*
  - SKEPTIC: *it's lying too neatly. Arms spread. Like it was placed.*
  - WEIRDO: *the dashboard clock says 7:41, and the second hand on your watch has stopped.*

**Beat 1: the first menu.** End the turn with a lettered menu. For example:
- **A.** Grab Dale's jacket. Nobody goes alone. All three of you go look.
- **B.** Stay in the van, doors locked, and try the ignition again.
- **C.** Let Dale go check, and watch him in the mirror.
- **D.** Other: type your own

```

```

**Beat 2: TROPE.** Whatever they choose, show TROPE moving for the first time. Letting Dale go alone (or going alone themselves) raises it; going together, or saying out loud *"we are not doing the thing"*, keeps it at 0.

```
[ TIP · TROPE (0 to 5) is how much you're acting like someone in a horror movie. Splitting up, investigating noises, saying "I'll be right back": all +1. Saying the sensible thing out loud and doing it: -1. High TROPE gets you noticed. ]
```

**What's in the road:** a scarecrow. Straw, a burlap face, a rotted farm coat. Just a scarecrow, fallen off a truck. Relief, jokes. Wendell: *"Classic false alarm. Next comes the real scare."* When they look back at the road a second later, from the van or walking back to it, **the scarecrow is gone.** There's only a trail of straw leading into the trees. (It was a Committee prop that Lyle planted, and Lyle took it back. But it doesn't feel like that.)

**Beat 3: the first roll.** The van won't start. *Of course* the van won't start. An **easy d20 (DC 8)**, shown openly, to get it going (pumping the gas, a Jock's shove down the hill, a Skeptic noticing the choke is out, a Weirdo saying please to it).

```
[ TIP · Easy things just happen. When it really matters, the d20 decides how well. +2 when it fits who you are. ]
```

A miss means the van starts anyway, after a long, very uncomfortable minute in which something walks across the road in the fog behind them, slowly, carrying a lantern. Don't describe it clearly.

**Beat 4: the move.** Something thumps on the roof of the van as it pulls away (it's a pinecone; or is it?). Wendell points at the player: *"Okay. This is literally your thing."* Let the move work, cleanly and funnily (the Jock holds the sliding door shut against the wind; the Nerd's *I've Seen This One* explains that the thing on the roof is always a pinecone in the first act; the Skeptic's *That's Fake* finds the pinecone; the Weirdo's *Old Ways* hangs a bell from the mirror). This one is free: the move is ready again for the rest of Act I.

**Beat 5.** Headlights ahead: a gas station sign buzzing in the fog. *LAST CHANCE GAS & BAIT.* The needle's on empty. Print the first status line.

## 1.1 THE LAST CHANCE GAS & BAIT

8:05 p.m. One pump, a soda machine, a rack of live bait, a pumpkin on every surface, and a hand-painted sign: *HOLLOW FEST TONIGHT! HAYRIDE! CORN MAZE! STAY ALL NIGHT!*

Behind the counter is **Earl** (`characters/npcs.md`): seventies, overalls, a toothpick. He looks at the van, then at them, sighs, glances **down at something under the counter**, and delivers it in a flat, rehearsed voice:

*"You kids ain't goin' up to Crescent Lake, are ya? ... Folks go up there on Halloween, and they don't... come... back."* Pause. He glances down again. *"...The old man in the woods walks at night. Turn back while you still can."* Another glance. *"...Gas is a dollar oh-nine."*

- **The cards** (`DISCOVER_EARL_CARDS`): he's reading it off index cards taped under the counter, in tidy handwriting, with stage directions (*"(pause)"*, *"(look at the dark woods)"*). SKEPTIC SEES it at once. Anyone can lean over the counter, or catch him flipping a card. The last card says: *"(if they go anyway: say 'Then God help you.' Collect $40 and ham from the Committee Nov. 1.)"*
- **Going off script** (`SOCIAL_EARL`): if the player talks to Earl like a person (asks how long he's done this, notices he looks tired, offers to pay for his ham, calls out the cards kindly), he puts the cards down. He tells them **one true thing**, quietly: *"Stay together. Three's the number. Three together, he can't lay a finger on you. That's all I know, and that's more than I'm paid to say."* (This is the first clue to the Group rule, `game/puzzles.md`.) Bullying him gets the cards, and nothing else.
- Earl calls whoever's paying "the visitor". He doesn't mean anything by it. He means everything by it.

## 1.2 COURTNEY

A red Camaro sits by the air pump, hood up, steaming. Its owner is filming it. **Courtney Vance** (`characters/companions.md`), homecoming sash, leather jacket, a huge camcorder on her shoulder, REC light on. *"Don't mind me. This is B-roll."*

- Her car's dead (Dale can tell: the fan belt snapped). She's headed to **Crescent Lake**. Specifically: *"Cabin 13. I won a free weekend. From a flyer."* She holds it up. It's **identical** to Dale's.
- **The flyers** (`DISCOVER_FLYER`): side by side, the two flyers have the same date, the same cabin, the same photo of a sunny dock. And at the bottom of both, in type so small you need to squint: *"Hollow Pines Halloween Committee · Visitor Program · Est. 1979."* SKEPTIC SEES it instantly. Wendell: *"Two groups. One cabin. That's a double booking, and in the movies, a double booking means—"* Dale: *"It means more people for the party!"*
- **The choice** (a menu): let her into the van (`RECRUIT_COURTNEY`), help her fix the car and go separately, or leave her. Wendell objects, of course: *"Never pick up the stranger at the gas station. Rule nine."* She's not the killer. She's just extremely annoying, and brave.
- **Why she matters, said plainly by Dale:** *"Three's a crowd, four's a party."* (A group of three or more is the whole game, though they don't know that yet.)

## 1.3 THE LAKE ROAD (optional detours)

The road up to Crescent Lake: four miles of gravel through the pines, fog in the headlights, the radio playing the local station, **WHOL 1350**, where a cheerful announcer reads the Hollow Fest schedule, and every few seconds a different voice, very quiet, says *"come for the leaves"*. Options, each costing a little time (`world/hollow-pines.md`):

- **The glovebox.** Dale's glovebox is jammed full of old Hollow Pines flyers, ten years of them, every one for Cabin 13, every one for Halloween. Dale gets very quiet. With trust 1 or more he tells them why he really wanted to come (`DISCOVER_DALE_GRANDPA` can begin here, but it's only reported in Act II, when he tells the whole story).
- **Wendell's notebook.** He'll happily read out *THE RULES*: don't split up, don't say you'll be right back, don't read anything in Latin, don't go in the basement, don't trust the sheriff, the dog always lives (*"we don't have a dog"*). He's confident about all of it. He also flinches whenever anyone mentions a specific scene, and changes the subject (`DISCOVER_WENDELL_SECRET` later, with trust).
- **Stop at the overlook.** A clearing above the lake. Down in the valley the town of Hollow Pines is lit up for the festival, orange lights, music. Across the black water, on a hill above town, there's a field of tiny flickering lights. Hundreds of them. *"Pumpkins,"* says Courtney, filming. *"Somebody carved like a thousand pumpkins."* (It's the Patch. Don't explain.)
- **Talk.** It's Halloween, they're seventeen, and this is the last fall before everybody leaves. Let it be sweet for a minute.

## 1.4 CABIN 13

9:30 p.m. The road ends at a clearing on the lake. A log cabin with a sagging porch, a rusted number **13** on the door, and every single cliché: a rocking chair rocking by itself, a wind chime made of bones (they're plastic), and a light already on in an upstairs window. There's no upstairs on the map.

**Walking through the front door ends Act I.** Record `REACH_CABIN` (it's sent with everything else at the end) and fetch the Act II pack.

## Exceptions

- **They turn around and leave right now**, back down County Road 9: `NOPE` (`game/endings.md`, fetch `pack-end.md`). Make them earn it: the van stalls once more on the way out, and something taps on the back window. If they still go, they go. It's a real ending, and in its way it's the smartest one.
- **Someone is alone with TROPE 4 or more** (unlikely this early): Jack Hollow takes them. If it's the player, it's `I'LL BE RIGHT BACK`.
