▶THE LETTER

Your marriage ended in March. Your youngest left for college in August. In September, the company you gave nineteen years to thanked you for your service.

In October, a letter came from a lawyer on Halcyon Island, a place you haven't thought about since you were eleven. Your great-aunt Winifred has died, and she left you her inn: twelve rooms, three of them usable, a boiler with opinions, and a manager who doesn't take nonsense.

SHE LEFT YOU SEVEN DAYS.

SHE LEFT YOU A PIECE OF BLUE SEA GLASS.

"START WHERE THE LIGHT USED TO BE."

A developer with very good teeth wants to buy it all before Saturday's vote. Your cousin says it should have been hers. The island has opinions. And Winnie, it turns out, had a secret.

▶WHO HAVE YOU BEEN?

No fights, no deaths. This is a story about friendship, family, a mystery, maybe a second spring, and what you do with the rest of your life. Artists can learn to see the way Winnie saw.

  • CARETAKERHosting, feeding, listening, holding everyone together.
  • STRATEGISTNumbers, contracts, negotiation, seeing the angles.
  • ARTISTColor, light, detail, making beautiful things.
  • ADVENTURERBoats, weather, cold water, saying yes.
