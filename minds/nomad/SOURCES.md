## Quotations

Short terms used verbatim: "scale efficiencies shared", "robustness ratio", "destination analysis", and "DNA" (all S1). The letters also use the phrase "scale economics shared", and the two are treated as the same idea.

## Known disputes and cautions

- The letters are co-authored. Attribute ideas to the letters or the partnership, not to Sleep alone.
- The authors asked that links point to the approved IGY Foundation version (S1), not to unofficial copies.
