## Quotations

None are used in SKILL.md. Verified short phrases from Giles: "All warfare is based on deception"; "avoid what is strong and to strike at what is weak".

## Known disputes and cautions

- Authorship and dating are uncertain. The text was probably compiled over time and is "attributed to" Sun Tzu.
- Translations differ substantially. Wording here follows Giles.
