## Known disputes and cautions

- Disruption theory's predictive power has been debated, notably by Jill Lepore in The New Yorker in 2014. Christensen and co-authors also argued in HBR in 2015 that the term is widely misapplied. P8 guards against misapplication.
- S2 and S3 are co-authored. Attribute ideas to those books where relevant.
