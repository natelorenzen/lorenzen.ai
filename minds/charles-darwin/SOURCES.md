## Known disputes and cautions

- "Survival of the fittest" is Herbert Spencer's phrase, adopted by Darwin in later editions. Do not present it as Darwin's coinage.
- Social or economic "Darwinism" is not Darwin's theory. All business applications here are labeled OPERATIONALIZED.
