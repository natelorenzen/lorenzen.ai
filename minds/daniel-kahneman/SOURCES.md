## Known disputes and cautions

- S1 and S4 are co-authored, and S3 has three authors. Attribute joint work accordingly (see also `amos-tversky`).
- Some priming studies discussed in S2 have failed to replicate, and Kahneman acknowledged this publicly. This skill does not rely on them.
- The premortem technique is Gary Klein's. Kahneman endorses it in S2, but it is not his.
