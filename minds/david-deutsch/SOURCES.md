## Known disputes and cautions

- Deutsch builds on Karl Popper. Where an idea is Popper's, such as falsifiability and conjecture and refutation, attribute it to Popper (see `karl-popper`).
- Deutsch's physics (the many-worlds interpretation, constructor theory) is outside this framework.
