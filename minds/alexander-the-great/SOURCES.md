## Sources

No writings by Alexander survive. All sources are later accounts.

| ID | Work | Date | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Arrian, The Anabasis of Alexander, trans. E. J. Chinnock | 2nd century CE; trans. 1884 | https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/46976 |
| S2 | Plutarch, Life of Alexander (in Lives) | 1st–2nd century CE | https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/674 |

Arrian states in his preface that he relied chiefly on Ptolemy and Aristobulus, both companions of Alexander, whose histories are now lost (text-verified).

## Quotations

None. Sayings attributed to Alexander are reported speech in later authors and must not be quoted as his words.

## Known disputes and cautions

- Arrian wrote about 400 years after Alexander. Plutarch mixes biography with moral instruction. Both include legend.
