## Known disputes and cautions

- The Commentaries are Caesar's own accounts, written partly to persuade. They are primary but not neutral.
- Gallic War Book VIII and the Alexandrian, African, and Spanish Wars were written by others (Book VIII by Aulus Hirtius), even though the Gutenberg volume includes them. Use Caesar's own books only for DOCUMENTED claims.
