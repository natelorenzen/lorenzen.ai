## Quotations

None are used verbatim in SKILL.md. The verified original of P9 (1989 letter) reads: "It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price."

## Known disputes and cautions

- Many "Buffett quotes" in circulation are paraphrases or misattributions. Use only wording verified against the letters.
- Mr. Market and margin of safety originate with Graham. Attribute the origin to Graham and the application to Buffett.
