## Quotations

SKILL.md uses no verbatim quotation other than the short phrase "lollapalooza effects" (S1). "Invert, always invert" is Munger quoting Jacobi (S3) and is not used as a Munger quotation.

## Known disputes and cautions

- The transcripts are third-party hosted. S4 contains revised versions, and the wording differs between editions.
- Many online "Munger quotes" are unverified. Use S1 to S4 only.
