## Known disputes and cautions

- Shannon himself warned against loose application of information theory beyond its domain ("The Bandwagon," IRE Transactions on Information Theory, 1956). All applications to organizations are labeled OPERATIONALIZED.
- A 1952 Bell Labs talk on "creative thinking" is often cited, but its text was not consulted, so it is not used.
