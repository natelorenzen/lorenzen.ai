Experiment Rules

Portfolio

$100,000 synthetic portfolio divided equally across ten candidates. Each position begins with a $10,000 allocation.

Entry

Cohort #1 is frozen in advance. Entry prices are based on the September 8, 2026 regular-session closing prices.

Selection

A hit occurs only if S&P Dow Jones Indices officially announces the company as an addition to the S&P 500.

Exit

Selected companies are synthetically sold at the next regular-session close after the inclusion announcement.

Benchmark

Performance is compared against SPY over the same holding period.

What we're testing

Whether identifying likely additions before announcement produces useful excess returns, and whether those returns actually come from the inclusion event.

Unfunded Watchlist

These companies are tracked for ranking diagnostics but receive no synthetic capital in Cohort #1.
