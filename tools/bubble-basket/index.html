Experiment rule

  1. Freeze ten candidates before the September 8 entry.
  2. Buy $10,000 of each synthetically at the September 8 official close.
  3. Make no substitutions before the next regular S&P 500 quarterly announcement.
  4. If S&P announces a basket member for inclusion, sell synthetically at the next regular-session close.
  5. Record total trade profit and the announcement-day component separately.

What counts as success?

The primary statistic is basket return versus SPY over the same period. Secondary statistics are candidate hit rate and event profit generated by selected stocks. Separating pre-announcement return from announcement return prevents ordinary stock momentum from being mislabeled as index-inclusion alpha.

Hypothesis: a diversified basket of probable additions earns positive SPY-relative returns, with a measurable portion arriving around successful inclusion announcements.
