Reading K-factor

K measures the next generation of customers created by each acquired customer through pass-alongs such as samples, referral cards, codes, gifts, or direct invites.

K < 0.5Helpful referral lift. Paid acquisition still drives most growth.
K = 0.5–1.0Strong virality. Referral growth materially improves acquisition economics.
K ≥ 1.0Each generation can replace or exceed itself under idealized assumptions.
All calculations run privately in your browser. Recursive virality is a theoretical model and should be treated as a scenario, not a forecast.
