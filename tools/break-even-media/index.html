How the math works

The calculator estimates contribution available before advertising. Desired profit is reserved before the remaining dollars become allowable acquisition cost.

  • Net revenue = AOV × (1 − refund rate)
  • Gross profit = net revenue × gross margin
  • Pre-ad contribution = gross profit − processing − fulfillment − variable costs
  • Break-even CPA = pre-ad contribution − desired profit

Gross margin should exclude advertising costs. Processing is calculated on gross order value.
