CTR improves CPC, CPA, and ROAS. Conversion rate improves CPA and ROAS. AOV improves ROAS without changing media efficiency above the purchase.

Equations and assumptions

Everything is normalized to 1,000 impressions. This isolates unit economics from budget and campaign scale. The calculator treats CPM, CTR, conversion rate, average order value, and gross margin as independent inputs.

  • Spend per 1,000 impressions = CPM
  • CPC = CPM ÷ (1,000 × CTR)
  • CPA = CPM ÷ (1,000 × CTR × conversion rate)
  • ROAS = 1,000 × CTR × conversion rate × AOV ÷ CPM
  • Contribution per 1,000 = revenue per 1,000 × gross margin − CPM
All calculations run locally in your browser. Results are planning estimates.
