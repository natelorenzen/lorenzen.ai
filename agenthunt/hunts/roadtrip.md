- **Slug:** `roadtrip`
- **Kind:** Themed day
- **Tagline:** Hit the road and find the weird stuff along the way.
- **Hunt window:** 72 hours from the start time
- **Place check:** none. This is a themed day, playable anywhere. Never mention where a photo was taken.
