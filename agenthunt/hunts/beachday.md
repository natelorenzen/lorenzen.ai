# AGENTHUNT · BEACH DAY

Hunt file for `#beachday`. The judging rules are in `https://lorenzen.ai/agenthunt/agenthunt.md`; follow them. This file gives the hunt's finds, window and area.

- **Slug:** `beachday`
- **Kind:** Themed day
- **Tagline:** Sand, salt and a sunset to finish.
- **Hunt window:** 24 hours from the start time
- **Place check:** none. This is a themed day, playable anywhere. Never mention where a photo was taken.
- **Best possible score:** 2,750

## Finds

| # | ID | Points | Find | What counts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `SANDCASTLE` | 200 | A sandcastle | A castle built in sand. A moat or towers earns a GREAT. |
| 2 | `SEASHELL` | 100 | A seashell | A shell found on the beach, held in a hand or lying in the sand. |
| 3 | `LIFEGUARD_TOWER` | 150 | A lifeguard tower | A lifeguard stand or tower. |
| 4 | `BREAKING_WAVE` | 150 | A breaking wave | A wave caught as it curls or crashes. |
| 5 | `SAND_PRINTS` | 100 | Footprints in the sand | A trail of footprints, paw prints or bird tracks. |
| 6 | `BEACH_UMBRELLA` | 100 | A beach umbrella | An umbrella planted in the sand. |
| 7 | `OCEAN_SUNSET` | 250 | A sunset over the water | The sun low over the water, or the sky glowing just after. |
| 8 | `SEABIRD` | 150 | A seabird | A gull, pelican or other seabird. |
| 9 | `BEACH_KITE` | 250 | A kite in the air | A kite flying over the beach. |
| 10 | `TIDE_POOL` | 300 | A tide pool creature | A crab, starfish, snail or anemone in a tide pool. Look, don't take. |

GREAT earns the full points, GOOD earns half. +50 on a find that also shows the run's proof detail. +500 for all 10 finds.

**Safety:** remind the player once to look, not take, with tide-pool creatures, and to watch the tide.

Show the player the finds as a numbered list (title and points only, no IDs), then continue with §3 of the judging rules.
