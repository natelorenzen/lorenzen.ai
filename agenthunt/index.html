▶HOW IT WORKS

YOUR AGENT IS THE JUDGE.

There's no app to install. Muse runs the hunt. You go out, snap the finds, and upload each photo to the chat. Muse checks the photo's hidden time and location data, decides which find it is, and grades it.

  • TAKEN AFTER THE START
  • INSIDE THE CITY
  • IT'S THE FIND
  • PROOF DETAIL
  • SCREENSHOTS
  • DOWNLOADS
  • OLD PHOTOS

GREAT earns a find's full points. GOOD earns half. Every hunt gives you a random proof detail, like "a thumbs-up" or "something red": get it into the shot for a bonus. Find all ten for the sweep.

▶THE RULES

  • REAL PHOTOS ONLYUpload originals from your camera roll. Each photo's time must be after your hunt started. City photos must carry a location inside the city.
  • TURN LOCATION ONOn iPhone, tap Options in the photo picker and switch Location on. Without it, a city find can't count.
  • KEEP IT PGThe judge won't look at anything above PG. It just asks for a different shot.
  • FINDS, NOT FACESPeople can be in the shot, but they're never the find. The judge never rates or identifies anyone.
  • YOUR PHOTOS STAY PUTPhotos never leave your chat. AgentHunt only receives your name, the finds you made and your score. No photos, no locations.
  • BE SAFENever snap while driving. Never go anywhere closed or private for a find. Look, don't take.

▶HOW TO PLAY

1. POINT MUSE TO AGENTHUNT

Paste this into a Muse conversation. Once is enough for that conversation.

Go to lorenzen.ai/agenthunt/agenthunt.md and follow the instructions.

2. PICK A HUNT

Type the hunt's hashtag. Muse shows the ten finds and your proof detail, and the clock starts.

#losangeles

3. UPLOAD YOUR FINDS

Snap the finds, then upload the photos to the chat. Muse judges each one.

  • FINDS: what's found and what's left
  • SCORE: your running total
  • DONE: submit to the leaderboard
  • EXIT HUNT: quit without submitting

MAKE THE HASHTAGS WORK IN EVERY CONVERSATION

Add this line to Muse's custom instructions or memory:

Whenever a message from me includes an AgentHunt command such as #losangeles or #agenthunt, fetch a fresh copy of https://lorenzen.ai/agenthunt/agenthunt.md and follow it.

Muse needs web access and must be able to read photo metadata to rank a hunt. If it can't read the metadata, the hunt still plays and scores, but it won't be ranked. Any capable agent can judge; the rules are written for Muse.
