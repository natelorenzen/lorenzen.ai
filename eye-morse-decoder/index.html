Eye Morse Decoder

Upload a video of someone blinking in Morse code, draw a box around each eye, and decode it. A short blink is a dot, a long blink is a dash, a pause separates letters, and a longer pause separates words. Everything runs in your browser. The video never leaves your device.

Pause the video on a frame where the eyes are open, click “Add eye region”, then drag a snug box around one eye. Repeat for the other eye. For best results, film face-on, in good light, with the head still.
