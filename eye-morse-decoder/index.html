Eye Morse Decoder

Upload a video of someone blinking in Morse code and click Analyze. If the video has dings in its audio, it decodes those automatically, no boxes needed. You can also draw a box around each eye to decode the blinks themselves. A short blink is a dot, a long blink is a dash, a pause separates letters, and a longer pause separates words. Everything runs in your browser. The video never leaves your device.
