Eye Morse Decoder

Upload a screen recording of the blinking eye and click Analyze. The tool finds the blinking eye by itself, reads every time it closes (a short close is a dot, a long close is a dash), and decodes the Morse. The countdown at the start and the dim eye at the end are ignored. Everything runs in your browser; the video never leaves your device.
